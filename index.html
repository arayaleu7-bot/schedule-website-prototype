Data source

Sample data is built in. To try your own catalog, paste a JSON array of courses (use "Insert template" for the format). Only use data you have permission to use. ScheduleWise never asks for your Cal Poly login and can't fetch official data on its own; custom data stays in your browser and is unverified.

Upload your DPR

Upload your Degree Progress Report (PDF, TXT, CSV or HTML) or paste its text. ScheduleWise reads it in your browser and shows what you’ve completed, what’s in progress, and what you still need. The file is never uploaded. Only the matched course codes are kept in this browser so your tracker remembers them. Never share your Cal Poly login.
