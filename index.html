Data source

Sample data is built in. To try your own catalog, paste a JSON array of courses (use "Insert template" for the format). Only use data you have permission to use. ScheduleWise never asks for your Cal Poly login and can't fetch official data on its own; custom data stays in your browser and is unverified.

Add courses from catalog text

Open a department’s course list at catalog.calpoly.edu/courses, select and copy the course descriptions, and paste them here. ScheduleWise adds each course’s code, title, units, GE area, term and prerequisite text as “catalog only” entries. Everything stays in your browser.

Add classes from a department schedule export

Upload or paste a department schedule export (tab-separated columns: Name, Alias, Title, Phone, Office, Office Hours, Course, Sect, Type, Days, Start, End, Location). ScheduleWise reads the real days, times, professors and rooms. These exports don’t list seats, units, titles or ratings, so sections show “seats not listed” and count as open. Lectures and labs become separate entries so you can add each one. Everything stays in your browser. If you host the site yourself, save the export as schedule.txt next to index.html and it loads automatically.

Upload your DPR

Upload your Degree Progress Report (PDF, TXT, CSV or HTML) or paste its text. ScheduleWise reads it in your browser and shows what you’ve completed, what’s in progress, and what you still need. The file is never uploaded, and your name and student ID are never read or kept. The course lists it finds are saved in this browser until you press Clear. It works for any major or college. Never share your Cal Poly login.

Schedule Builder

Section days, times, rooms and professors come from the Spring 2027 department schedule exports for every college (unofficial). Seats, ratings and workload aren’t in that data, so they show as not listed. Entries marked "Catalog only" use course codes, titles and units from the published 2026-28 GE lists and have no section times. Sample classes now cover departments for every major so you can try the tools. Use the Data panel to add real classes from the catalog.
